# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Israeli social media managers, Facebook/Instagram campaign runners (קמפיינרים), and business owners whose entire lead flow comes from paid social. Predominantly mobile. They arrive from Facebook ads, often late at night, already carrying background anxiety about losing their accounts. Many have either experienced account suspension themselves or know someone who has. They're not technical — they just want to sleep without worrying about Meta.

## Product Purpose

BMS is a ₪197 course teaching the correct setup and ongoing protection of Meta Business Manager: 4 core modules, 3 bonuses, 15 recorded lessons. The course exists because people don't get blocked randomly — there's a formula, and this course teaches how to stay outside it. The author (Osher Ravach / IsraelTechForce) built his reputation recovering 2,500+ accounts; the course is the next step, prevention instead of rescue. Success means a user who finishes the page thinks: "I need this now, and I trust this person to teach it."

## Brand Personality

אמין, מקצועי, ישיר (Trustworthy, Professional, Direct). Osher is a straight-talking expert who has seen every variation of this problem. The page should feel like a knowledgeable friend who calls it exactly as it is — no hype, no fluff, no urgency theater. Authority earned through specificity, not through design drama.

## Emotional Arc (First 3 Seconds)

Two feelings must land simultaneously: "זה בדיוק בשבילי" (personal recognition — the page is speaking to my exact situation) and "אני בסכנה וצריך לפתור" (urgency — I didn't realize how exposed I am). The design and copy work together to surface the pain before offering the solution.

## Anti-references

- **"Guru" funnels**: Yellow/orange countdown-timer pages with aggressive testimonial stacks, infomercial energy. This is not that. No fake urgency, no blinking elements.
- **Generic Israeli digital-marketing course pages**: Canva-looking layouts, stock photos of laptops on desks, templated typography, generic "professional" feel with no personality.
- **Over-designed tech landing pages**: Three.js animations, particle effects, excessive interactivity. Heavy ≠ trustworthy. Converts worse, loads worse.

## Design Principles

1. **Mirror before selling.** The page earns the right to pitch by naming the exact pain first. Personal recognition ("זה קרה לי") precedes authority claims.
2. **Osher is the brand.** This is a personal brand page. The human, the story, the face carry more weight than design polish. Design supports the person, not the other way around.
3. **Direct is a feature.** ישיר means the design clears the path for the copy. No decorative noise, no visual complexity that competes with the message.
4. **Calm urgency.** The user is stressed. The design should feel composed and controlled — not chaotic or loud — while still making the stakes clear.
5. **Authority through specificity.** Credibility comes from concrete details (2,500 cases, specific names, specific failures) not from design bravado (glow effects, hero metrics).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA standard. RTL Hebrew throughout. Mobile-first (majority of traffic is mobile, from Facebook ads). Reduced motion preference must be respected.
